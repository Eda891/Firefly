Who we are

One screen, run by people who watch every film twice

Firefly opened in 2019 in a converted print shop on Mill Row. We only ever show one film a night, so every screening gets a full house's worth of attention — proper projection, a real intermission, and someone at the door who's actually seen the movie.

We program a mix of restored prints, first-run indies, and local shorts nights. No pre-show ads, no assigned popcorn combos — just a small room that takes film seriously.
